    How to play

    Accelerate / brake
    W S or ↑ ↓
    Steer
    A D or ← →
    Use item
    Space
    Handbrake
    Shift — hold it into a corner and the tail comes round
    Pause
    Esc or P

    A gamepad works too: left stick or d-pad to steer, RT or A for the loud pedal, LT to brake, X or Y to fire, RB for the handbrake and Start to pause. The menus take the d-pad, A to choose and B to go back.

    On a phone

    The car drives itself. Slide a thumb anywhere on the left to steer — the further it slides, the harder you turn. Hold anywhere on the right for the handbrake and drift the corner. ★ fires your item.

    Items

    • TURBO A short, glorious overdose of speed.
    • ROCKET Fires forward and nudges toward whoever is ahead.
    • OIL DRUM Drops behind you. Whoever hits it, spins.

    Grass and dirt will slow you down and steal your grip. Barriers hurt more.
